Getting a birthday gift for Mom

Preparation
1. Check the calendar and set a deadline. Note the birthday date and any time you need to account for (shipping, event planning).
2. Set a budget. Decide how much you want to spend, and whether you’ll include shipping or a card.
3. Think about Mom’s preferences. Jot down things she likes (hobbies, colors, favorite scents, authors, activities), things she needs or has mentioned wanting, and any restrictions (allergies, space limits).
4. Decide the type of gift you want to give: a physical item, an experience (dinner, class, outing), or something handmade/personal.

Decide what to give
5. Brainstorm 5–10 specific gift ideas using your notes (e.g., cozy blanket, cooking class, personalized photo book, spa kit, handmade scarf).
6. Narrow to top 2–3 options. Consider meaning, usefulness, and how likely she is to enjoy each.
7. Check logistics for each option: availability in stores, shipping times, cost, and whether you can get it personalized.

Buy or make the gift
8. If buying in store:
   - Find nearby shops that carry the item.
   - Check hours and any stock availability online or by phone.
   - Go purchase the item and keep the receipt and any warranty information.
9. If ordering online:
   - Compare reputable retailers for price and delivery date.
   - Read delivery and return policies, and check reviews.
   - Order with enough lead time; choose expedited shipping if needed.
   - Save order confirmations and tracking information.
10. If making the gift by hand:
    - List materials and tools needed.
    - Buy supplies with time to spare.
    - Allow extra time for crafting, drying, or finishing.
    - If including a recipe or instructions (e.g., baked goods), test or plan timing so it’s fresh on the day.

Wrap and add a personal touch
11. Choose wrapping or presentation. Pick gift wrap, a gift bag, or a box that suits the gift.
12. Add a personal note. Write a short, heartfelt card or letter highlighting specific reasons you appreciate her.
13. Add small extras if appropriate (flowers, a favorite treat, a photo, or a printed schedule for an experience).

Plan how and when to give it
14. Decide the moment: at a family gathering, during a quiet one-on-one, or via delivery. Coordinate with others if it’s a group surprise.
15. If the gift is an experience, confirm reservations or tickets and make sure logistics (transportation, timing) are arranged.
16. If you’ll be away, plan reliable delivery or arrange someone to present the gift in person.

Final steps and review
17. Double-check everything two or three days before the birthday: gift arrival or readiness, wrapping, card, and timing.
18. Keep the receipt and note return or exchange deadlines.
19. After giving the gift, observe her reaction and follow up later to make sure she’s happy with it. If needed, handle returns or exchanges promptly.
20. Reflect briefly: what went well and what you might do differently next time (useful for future gift planning).

Short tips
- For last-minute needs: consider a digital gift card, a subscription, a same-day delivery service, or a heartfelt, handwritten coupon for time together.
- Personalization increases meaning: small custom touches (initials, favorite scent, a shared memory) often matter more than cost.
